# Code of Conduct

We want project spaces to be useful and safe for contributors from construction,
building operations, design, research, and software backgrounds.

Be respectful, critique ideas rather than people, explain specialist terms, and
do not pressure anyone to disclose employer, client, tenant, or personal data.
Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, and publication of private job data
are not acceptable.

Project maintainers may edit or remove content and restrict participation when
needed to protect contributors or the public. Until a private conduct address is
published, report a concern to the maintainer without repeating sensitive
material in a public issue.
