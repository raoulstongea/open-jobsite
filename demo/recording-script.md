## Truth checklist before publishing

- Use a fresh synthetic data directory and show no private paths, keys, names, or notifications.
- Do not splice a scripted preflight result into footage presented as a live agent run.
- Keep failed or retried tool calls visible unless the caption clearly discloses the edit.
- Confirm the final values against `expected-result.md`.
- Label the client accurately: goose Desktop, goose CLI, or Berd.
